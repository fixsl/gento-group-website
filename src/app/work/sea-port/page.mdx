## Overview

An ambitious, long-term infrastructure initiative, the Gento Seaport Project involves the construction of a 22-meter deep-sea transshipment port on Banana Island.
This port is designed to become a regional maritime hub, connecting West Africa to global shipping lanes.
The significant depth allows for the berthing of the world’s largest vessels, enhancing Sierra Leone’s competitiveness in international trade.
